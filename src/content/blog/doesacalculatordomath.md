My second day with MindsEmerge I sat behind my keyboard and thought, "I don't think I've ever really experienced <span class="blog-accent-warn">imposter syndrome</span> before. So this is how that feels". I tapped away at the buttons on my laptop, creating a simple wireframe in Figma, feeling like I was <span class="blog-accent-warn">wasting their time, wasting my time</span>. After a few minutes, I decided, what the hell, let's give <span class="blog-accent-good">Claude Design </span>another shot.

I'd tried Claude Design before and I have to say I was not impressed. Better results seemed to come from building a design system first, or at least an outline of an idea, before feeding that into Claude Code to quickly generate a front end mock-up.

This time I had branding guidelines, a clear idea of what I wanted, <span class="blog-accent-warn">but no design system yet</span>. In less than 10 minutes it assembled a cogent and pleasant design system based on the provided color palette and logo. With a little bit of feedback, a dashboard was starting to take form. Over the next few hours and into the evening (*I couldn't stay away off hours*) I had my first mock-up to a satisfying state.

I've been asking myself a few questions:
1. How do I <span class="blog-accent-warn">use AI safely</span> and efficiently to increase my productivity?
2. How do I make <span class="blog-accent-good">my mark</span> on what an AI produces?
3. What can I call <span class="blog-accent-good">my work</span>?

I don't have any answers yet, but I'm hoping to find common community to discuss these questions and more as the <span class="blog-accent-good">Ann Arbor SPARK Digital Summer Clinic</span> meets throughout the summer.

-Anthony
