This is the first post on a freshly built blog. I wanted a place to write that wasn't a tweet, wasn't a LinkedIn essay, and wasn't a case study in disguise. Mostly I wanted a place where I could think out loud about the work I'm doing, the work I want to do, and the projects I'm building when I'm not working.

The plan is to keep these posts short. A few paragraphs, not three thousand words. If I have something longer to say, it'll probably end up as a case study instead.

Topics I expect to write about:

- Things I'm building (the Seed Library buildout, weird side experiments)
- Things I'm reading (mostly books and field studies, occasionally a paper)
- Things I'm thinking about as a designer-developer (which, increasingly, is the same job)
- Things I'm cooking (low effort, high return)

Thanks for reading.
